Sweden's Azure Community

Why Azure User Group Sweden?

Since 2017, our group has hosted talks, labs, and meetups that showcase real stories from the Azure ecosystem. We amplify the voices of local experts while collaborating with Microsoft to highlight global innovations.

Expect curated sessions, honest discussions, and hands-on experiences tailored for the cloud-first professional. Whether you are just starting or architecting at scale, you're welcome here.

Community Highlights

  • Monthly Meetups Hybrid events with experts from Scandinavia and beyond.
  • Hands-on Workshops Guided labs to explore Azure AI, DevOps, Data, Security, and FinOps.
  • Global Reach Livestreams, recordings, and resources to support the wider Azure community.
