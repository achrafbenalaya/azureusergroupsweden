About Azure User Group Sweden

We are a grassroots community amplifying Sweden's Azure stories. Our focus is collaboration, knowledge sharing, and creating a welcoming entry point into the global Azure ecosystem.

Our Story

Powered by passionate practitioners

Azure User Group Sweden started as a small meetup in Stockholm where a handful of cloud engineers shared lessons learned. We have since grown into a national community with hybrid events that connect architects, developers, data scientists, and IT professionals.

Our mission is to demystify Azure technologies, promote inclusive learning, and spotlight the work of Swedish organizations adopting cloud innovation. Every session is rooted in real-world experience.

Part of Microsoft's Azure Tech Groups

As an officially recognized Azure Tech Group, we collaborate closely with Microsoft Reactor teams and product groups to bring early insights, roadmap discussions, and speakers to Sweden. This partnership helps us amplify local voices worldwide while staying aligned with platform best practices.

Membership is free, events are recorded whenever possible, and content is selected through community feedback cycles.

Community Timeline

A snapshot of how the Azure User Group has expanded its impact across Sweden.

2017 - First Meetup

Launched in Stockholm with an interactive session on migrating to Azure App Service.

2019 - Going Hybrid

Introduced livestreams to include members from Gothenburg, Malmö, and beyond.

2021 - Azure Tech Group Recognition

Became an official Microsoft Azure Tech Group, unlocking deeper collaborations.

2023 - Community Workshops

Expanded into hands-on labs featuring Azure AI, DevOps, and data modernization tracks.
